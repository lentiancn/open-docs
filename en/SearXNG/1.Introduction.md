# SearXNG Introduction

SearXNG is a free internet metasearch engine that aggregates results from up to 250 search services. Users are neither tracked nor profiled, ensuring complete privacy protection.

## Key Features

- **Privacy First**: No user tracking or profiling
- **Metasearch Engine**: Aggregates results from 250+ search services
- **Anonymity Support**: Can be used over Tor for online anonymity
- **Open Source**: Fully open source and community-driven
- **Customizable**: Highly configurable for personal or organizational use

## Getting Started

You can start using SearXNG immediately by visiting public instances listed at [searx.space](https://searx.space). For maximum privacy and control, consider setting up your own private instance.

## Privacy Protection Levels

SearXNG implements three levels of data removal to protect user privacy:

1. **From requests to search services**: Removes identifying information before sending queries
2. **From third-party services**: Blocks tracking scripts and analytics
3. **From result pages**: Sanitizes URLs and removes tracking parameters

This comprehensive approach ensures that your search activity remains completely private and anonymous.
